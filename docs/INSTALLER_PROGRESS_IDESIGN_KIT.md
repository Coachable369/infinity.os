# InfinityOS Installation Progress IDesign Kit

The installation-progress screen follows the supplied 2006 × 784 reference as its measurable composition target. The generated background plate preserves the complete HUD frame, ultrawide horizon, central hero viewport, illuminated infinity emblem, side inscriptions, footer marks, and empty lower glass console.

## Live layers

- The status label and percentage are framebuffer text driven by verified installer progress.
- The progress track, fill, quarter markers, and active flares are rendered for every reported percentage.
- A moving star glint follows the infinity path over the illuminated emblem independently of progress.
- The mouse cursor and all progress values are excluded from the background artwork.

## Reference geometry

- Hero viewport: approximately 17–83% horizontal and 9–62% vertical.
- Progress console: approximately 11–89% horizontal and 67–86% vertical.
- Status baseline: approximately 71% vertical.
- Progress track: approximately 14–86% horizontal and 77% vertical.
- Palette: near-black navy glass, restrained cyan outlines, ice-blue fill, and white-blue highlights.

The progress plate is installer-only. It is embedded in live ISO kernels and is not copied into the installed desktop payload.
