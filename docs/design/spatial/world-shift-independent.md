# Independent World Shift surfaces

World Shift and Holographic Desktop are independent system surfaces. They are
launched from their own application tiles; Holographic Desktop also remains
available from Shift-Tab and Command/Super-Tab. Spatial Desktop exposes only
Gravity Wall, Matter Shelf, and Constellations as its local tabs.

Themes & Skins begins with a full-width World Shift gateway. The gateway and
launcher tile route to the same World Shift controller and therefore share the
same durable world layouts, names, appearance snapshots, navigator locations,
and editor state.

## Visual contract

`worldshift-hero-v1` is the source-of-truth campaign image. Its four portal
worlds correspond in order to `spatial-world-0` through `spatial-world-3`.
Each 1672 x 941 background was generated by expanding its matching portal from
the hero rather than by producing an unrelated scene. PNG sources are retained
for future art direction; 24-bit BMP runtime derivatives are embedded in both
live and freshly installed kernels.

World travel uses the retained two-scene compositor. The source desktop remains
frozen while the destination is restored and then crossfaded at the display
refresh cadence. Reduced-motion mode still completes the state change without
discarding application or document state.
