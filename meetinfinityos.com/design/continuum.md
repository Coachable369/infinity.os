# Continuum 02 / Motion-Led Design Contract

## Direction

The first flat, centered composition was rejected. Follow `IDesign-Kit.png`:
asymmetrical, full-bleed cinematic stage, concise left-aligned typography,
fine circuit traces and a sculptural cobalt emblem. No white brochure band.
The generated environment is an art-direction reference, not a product photo.
Do not add the board's fictional sign-in or version label.

## Brand

`emblem-reference.png` is decoded directly from `infinity-emblem-v2.bmp`.
Its same-named PNG companion is different artwork. The actual BMP pixels
remain authoritative for navigation, fallback and small brand marks.
The 3D interpretation must preserve the two broad rounded lobes, open centers
and foreground rising diagonal. No typographic infinity or torus knot.

## Motion

The user explicitly requested real axis rotation and animated electrical paths.
Use the actual artwork on a silhouette-traced beveled 3D relief, with blue-chrome
edge shading. The kit has no original 3D model; do not substitute a generic tube.
At rest it turns gently; native scrolling adds a larger reversible Y-axis turn
and modest X-axis tilt. Do not hijack scrolling or require drag input.
Circuit traces stay on the ground plane; bright pulses travel along their
lengths and branch outward. They are not decorative floating dots.
The hero should introduce the next section within the first viewport.

Pause freezes both idle rotation and electrical pulses. Reduced motion removes
idle and scroll-driven transforms. Offscreen/background scenes stop scheduling
frames. Static fallback must remain usable without WebGL or script.
The 3D library is a separate, pinned and self-hosted rendering dependency.
The user explicitly approved heavy frameworks for this redesign on 2026-10-20.

## Components

- Carbon `#050608`, platinum `#eff2f4`, cobalt `#2765ff`, cyan `#68e4ff`.
- Inter self-hosted; system monospace for short technical labels.
- Zero letter spacing. Fixed responsive type steps, not viewport-scaled fonts.
- Desktop H1 96px, tablet 76px, mobile 54px; lightweight supporting headline.
- Desktop gutter 64px, tablet 40px, mobile 24px.
- Header 76px desktop / 68px mobile. Real BMP-derived brand mark.
- 44px icon controls, Lucide symbols, accessible names, clear focus borders.
- Full-width page sections, unframed headings, thin separators.
- Screenshot previews reuse the original app-study frame, radius 6px maximum.
- Section links, keyboard navigation and FAQ remain fully usable.

## Acceptance

Capture actual desktop and mobile frames. Check no overflow or text/art overlap.
Prove canvas pixels change at rest and after scrolling, geometry has real depth,
electrical pulse position advances, and pause/reduced-motion stop the animation.
Check original logo derivative pixels, all image loads and console errors.

commercial paths and proposed diligence milestones from demonstrated outcomes.
