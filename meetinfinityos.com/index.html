What is InfinityOS?

A new operating system bringing a desktop workspace, everyday tools, and contextual AI together. Designed around the person using it, and the possibilities in front of them.

What makes it different?

Intelligence is part of the workspace itself. Voice, app-aware assistance, and creative tools are being designed together, with your intent at the center.

When can I experience it?

InfinityOS is in active development. Public availability and supported hardware will be shared here when they’re ready. Some things are worth getting right.
